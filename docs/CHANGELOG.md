# What's new

What has changed in harmONIc, newest first. Written for somebody using it
rather than somebody working on it, so a week of small corrections is one entry
and a fortnight of quiet is none.

Every rate here comes from the game's wiki, and the entries say when one of them
turned out to be wrong — because a planner that was wrong last week and does not
admit it is worse than one that never claimed to be right.

## 27 August 2026 — What the players found

A week of reports from the Oxygen Not Included Discord, and most of them were
right.

**The kitchen.** Every cooked dish is a material now rather than a number of
calories, so a Gas Range can take what an Electric Grill made and a Duplicant
eats a *meal*. 73 recipes across nine buildings, and all 64 calorie figures read
back against their source.

**Things that were wrong.** A Marine Drill made a quarter of the sulfur it
should and none of the natural gas. An Orehull was sheared for ten times too
much iron ore. A grazed Arbor Tree grew a tenth of what it grows, and a Pip made
half the dirt it makes — between them, a build that wanted 29 grazed trees now
wants a third of one. A Mixed Berry Pie was made without its grubfruit, Curried
Beans without any beans, and the Mush Bar fed eight hundred Duplicants.

**Things that were missing.** The Oakshell, whose molts are wood that costs no
water and which is what lets an ethanol loop close. The Cuddle Pip. The Polluted
Brine Vent. Kelpoles that something will actually take. Sublimation, where the
gas costs the thing that offgasses instead of appearing from nowhere.

**Two wires into one port** used to split that port evenly and not say so, which
quietly doubled a build. It says so now, and tells you what to do about it.

**Asking twice** for the least of something gave a different answer each time.
It does not any more.

**The palette folds.** 428 of its rows are the supply, output and eating node
that exist for every item, and they start out of the way.

## 26 August 2026 — Food

Plants grow crops rather than calories, so what happens to a crop is now
something you draw rather than something the app assumes. Eating is a node: one
per food, under EATING in the palette.

The Electric Grill, the Gas Range, the Deep Fryer, the Sushi Bar, the Smoker,
the Microbe Musher, the Dehydrator and the Rehydrator all know their recipes.
